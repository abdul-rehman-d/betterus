# BetterYou typography

The product already uses two open-source families under the SIL Open Font License.

- **Epilogue:** display headings and expressive brand moments. Use 600–700 weights.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** body copy, controls, labels, and data. Use 400–700 weights.
- Keep the written brand name as `BetterYou`, with no space, matching the app name.
- The symbol is the primary logo for launcher, avatar, splash, and compact product contexts. Pair it with a typeset `BetterYou` label only when recognition requires the name; do not lock the live UI font into the SVG master.
