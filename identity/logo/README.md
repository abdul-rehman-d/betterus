# BetterYou logo usage

## Idea

Two nested upward-open paths turn **Us** into the symbol: two people remain distinct while progressing in the same direction.

## Files

- `betterus-symbol.svg` — editable two-color master.
- `betterus-symbol-black.svg` — one-color master.
- `betterus-symbol-white.svg` — optically thinned reversed master.
- `betterus-app-icon.svg` — full-bleed 1024×1024 launcher source.
- `betterus-android-foreground.svg` — transparent adaptive foreground source.
- `betterus-android-monochrome.svg` — Android themed-icon source.
- `betterus-splash-symbol.svg` — light-surface splash source.

## Rules

- Keep clear space equal to one stroke width around the standalone symbol.
- Minimum digital size: 16 px for the one-color symbol; 24 px for the two-color symbol.
- Do not rotate, stretch, add effects, close the open ends, or recolor the two paths with unrelated hues.
- The app-icon source is full bleed; platform masks create its final corner shape.
- Android foreground artwork stays inside the centered 66/108 safe zone.

The SVG masters intentionally retain simple editable strokes. Expand strokes before sending artwork to vinyl cutting or embroidery equipment.

Trademark clearance is not included. Run a professional trademark and reverse-image search before registration or a major public launch.
