# BetterUs color system

The identity keeps the product palette already established in the app. Berry is the memory color; plum supplies premium contrast; petal and warm white keep the system intimate rather than clinical.

| Token | Hex | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| BetterUs Berry | `#B51E52` | 181, 30, 82 | Primary brand and inner path |
| Deep Berry | `#81143C` | 129, 20, 60 | App-icon depth and dark accents |
| Berry Light | `#D94B7C` | 217, 75, 124 | Highlight and gradient start |
| Plum | `#2A131E` | 42, 19, 30 | Outer path and primary text |
| Petal | `#FFD7E4` | 255, 215, 228 | Secondary path on dark fields |
| Warm White | `#FFF9FA` | 255, 249, 250 | Primary light surface/reversed mark |

## Logo combinations

- Default: Plum outer path + BetterUs Berry inner path on Warm White.
- App icon: Warm White outer path + Petal inner path on the Berry gradient.
- One color: solid black, Plum, Berry, or white. Never rely on color to distinguish the two paths.
- Avoid placing the two-color mark on photography without a quiet solid-color container.

The Pantone and CMYK equivalents remain provisional until a physical print proof is made.
